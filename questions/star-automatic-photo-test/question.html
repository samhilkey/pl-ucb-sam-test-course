Automatic photo recognition sandbox

Upload / Take photo puts your photograph on the canvas as an ungraded reference. Use Edit on canvas to draw, then add separate dots with Other marks. Only native drawings are graded. Use Review detected graph when calibration succeeds. Otherwise use Align / select graph ink: place the origin and known axis ticks, enter their values, then click each curve's two endpoints and any filled dots. Review and explicitly accept the selected ink as native objects. This correction is CV-assisted, not automatic recognition. Photo options retains advanced review and manual tracing.

Sketch $f(x)=x^2$. This sandbox keeps the native $x^2$ grader: an imported sample can be graded incorrect while faithfully preserving its original marks.
