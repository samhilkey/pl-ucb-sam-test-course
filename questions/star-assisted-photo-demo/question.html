Use a photo of your paper graph

Choose Trace a paper graph (manual) to place the axes, enter their reference values and trace each branch on the photograph. Review the orange trace before adding it.

Keep your original corners, endpoints, gaps and mistakes. After import, use Select to move a whole branch. If a trace needs a local correction, retrace it on the photo before importing.

This is a transcription sandbox with an x-squared target. The example paper graphs may correctly receive 0% while their original shapes are preserved. Automatic recognition is optional; use the manual route for this demonstration.
